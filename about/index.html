How we approach an ECA enquiry

1. Define the asset

Review material, dimensions, drawings, access, surface or tube condition, inspection quantity and location.

2. Match the method

Evaluate the target defect and whether surface ECA, tube ECA or another technique is suitable. Probe availability and procedure qualification are confirmed for the scope.

3. Agree the deliverable

Set coverage, applicable standard, calibration or reference specimen, reporting format, schedule and commercial responsibilities before execution.
